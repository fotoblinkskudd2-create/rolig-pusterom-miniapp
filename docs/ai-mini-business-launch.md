# AI Design Principles Mini-Business Launch

Output from the four-step launch workflow: concept, name and slogan, logo prompt, and launch post.

## 1. Business concept

**Eight AI Boys** is a small studio of eight specialised AI agents that build practical, working things for people who have an idea but no team. A customer describes a need, such as a weekly budget, a garden plan, or a small online shop, and the crew assembles it from modular parts: a planner, a writer, a designer, a checker, and so on. Each part is self-contained and can be swapped, reused, or snapped together with another.

- **Apple:** Every deliverable starts with one screen and one clear action. Customers see a single clean interface and a usable result within minutes, never the machinery behind it.
- **Lego:** The agents are modular. A pricing module built for one client becomes a building block for the next, so each project gets cheaper and faster to build.
- **Elon Musk:** The crew is built to be rebuilt. Each agent is reviewed and replaced on a short cycle. The company targets the slow, expensive parts of small-business work (bookkeeping, layout, copywriting) and aims to make them near-instant.
- **IKEA:** Transparent, fixed prices. Finished projects are delivered as a downloadable kit with plain instructions, designed so the customer can finish the last steps themselves and use it on a basic phone.

Early adopters get the first twenty projects at a founding price, and their feedback decides which module gets built next.
