## Legal flag

"We make it yours" and "where ideas become patents" imply patent prosecution. If the client doesn't file through registered patent attorneys or agents, legal should soften the end line to "Where ideas become patentable." A small super, *"Patent filings handled by registered practitioners"*, is cheap insurance.
